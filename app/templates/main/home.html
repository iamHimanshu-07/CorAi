What it does

①

Collect 11 features

Age, resting blood pressure, cholesterol, fasting blood sugar, max heart rate, ST depression (oldpeak), sex, chest pain type, resting ECG, exercise angina, ST slope.

②

Preprocess & calibrate

Numerics are standardized, categoricals are one-hot encoded. The classifier wrapping is isotonic-calibrated with 5-fold cross-validation so predicted probabilities are meaningful.

③

Score & band

The highest-CV-AUC model from Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, and LightGBM returns a probability (0–100%) and a Low / Moderate / High band.

④

Audit & explain

Every prediction is persisted with the input features, model version, and the user that triggered it. SHAP feature contributions are shown on the result page.
