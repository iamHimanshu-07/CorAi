About CorAi

An AI-driven medical analytics platform designed for early detection of cardiovascular risks, automated report extraction, and area-wise specialist recommendations.

🚀 What This Platform Does

📊 Predictive Cardiac Risk Scoring

Evaluates 11 key medical measurements (Age, Resting Blood Pressure, Cholesterol, Fasting Blood Sugar, Max Heart Rate, ST Depression, Sex, Chest Pain Type, Resting ECG, Exercise Angina, and ST Slope) using calibrated Machine Learning ensembles to output an instant risk score (Low, Moderate, High).

📄 Automated PDF Medical Report Extraction

Allows patients and doctors to upload PDF health reports. The system automatically extracts patient details, clinical metrics, and location, generating interactive comparison charts with baseline health standards.

📍 Area-Wise Doctor Recommendation Map

Integrates OpenStreetMap and Leaflet geospatial mapping to locate nearby verified cardiologists based on the patient's area extracted from their medical report.

💬 HeartAI Assistant (AI Copilot)

Embedded floating chatbot powered by OpenAI LLM architectures. Provides instant answers regarding heart disease prevention, dietary recommendations, exercise habits, and platform navigation.

🛠️ Technology Architecture

Machine Learning Scikit-Learn · XGBoost · LightGBM · SHAP Explainability · SMOTE Balancing
Web Framework Python 3.14 · Flask 3.0 · SQLAlchemy · SQLite · Gunicorn WSGI
Interactive Analytics Plotly.js Gauges & Bar Charts · PDFPlumber Text Extractor
Mapping & AI OpenStreetMap · Leaflet.js · OpenAI GPT Chat Completions

📖 How to Use the System

1
Upload PDF or Run Manual Prediction Navigate to Upload Report to submit a PDF lab result, or click New Prediction to manually enter your clinical data.
2
Review Risk Analysis & Comparative Charts View your calculated probability, risk band, and Plotly charts comparing your cholesterol and blood pressure against standard baseline ranges.
3
Locate Nearby Cardiologists on Map Click Find Doctor or the map recommendation button to view verified specialists near your area with direct phone and email contact details.
4
Chat with HeartAI Copilot Use the floating Chat with HeartAI widget in the bottom right to ask any health or platform question at any time.
